Our causes

Six global causes, one local focus

Lions and Leos around the world work on the same six causes. We choose the work where Matara needs us most.

  • Children & youth
  • Environment & sustainability
  • Health & nutrition
  • Hunger & food security
  • Vision care
  • Disaster management

Leo clubs across Sri Lanka run projects in 21 categories, from education and literacy to community development. There’s room for whatever you’re good at.

Come and do something useful with us.

You don’t need experience. You need a few free hours and a wish to help. If you’re between 12 and 30 and live in or near Matara, tell us who you are and we’ll message you back.
