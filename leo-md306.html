Who they are

The Leos of Sri Lanka and the Maldives

Leo Multiple District 306 is the youth organisation of Lions Clubs International across Sri Lanka and the Maldives. It brings every Leo club in the two countries under one name, one set of values and one yearly plan.

The first club was chartered in Wattala in 1969, and the first Maldivian club followed in Malé in 2023. The Leo Club of Matara Excellence is one of the newer clubs in that family.

3

How it is organised

Three levels, one family

Leo work is planned at the top, coordinated in the middle and carried out by clubs. A project in Matara can draw on a district partner, a national campaign and a Lions mentor at the same time.

Multiple District 306

The national level. It sets the direction, the standards and the yearly theme for every club in Sri Lanka and the Maldives, and is led by an elected Multiple District President.

Twelve districts

Each district coordinates the clubs in its own region and keeps its own service calendar. Ours is Leo District 306 D8.

More than 210 clubs

Every club is run by its own members and sponsored by a Lions club. Officers serve from July to June, so every club gets a fresh team each year.

What they stand on

Leadership, Experience, Opportunity

Every club, in every district, works to the same three pillars. They describe what a young person gets from a Leo club: the chance to lead a team, the practice of doing the work, and the pathways and friendships that come afterwards.

L

Leadership

Plan projects, manage time and guide teams to outcomes that matter.

E

Experience

Learn by doing: collaborate, execute and see the impact of service on your community.

O

Opportunity

Make lifelong connections with purpose-driven people while growing your skills and influence.

21

Causes

Service in 21 categories

Leo clubs choose their projects from 21 categories of service, from health and education to the environment and community welfare. Six of them carry a global cause of Lions Clubs International. Every project is planned and run locally by the club that takes it on.
