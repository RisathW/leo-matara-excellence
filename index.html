Who we are

A new Leo club, and we’re just getting started.

We’re the Leo Club of Matara Excellence, part of Leo District 306 D8 and the youth side of Lions Clubs International. Leos are aged 12 to 30, and we run our own projects: we plan them, we fund them, and we turn up to do the work.

We exist for young people in Matara who want to do something useful with their free time, and would rather do it with friends.

History

It started with a baseball coach

The first Leo club began in 1957. Here is how we got from there to Matara.

Read our history →
  1. 1957

    The first Leo club

    A Pennsylvania Lion named Jim Graver, who coached baseball at Abington High School, starts a service club for young people after his son asks why there isn’t one.

  2. 1969

    Leos in Sri Lanka

    The movement reaches Sri Lanka and grows into Leo Multiple District 306, with over 210 clubs today.

  3. Today

    Leo Club of Matara Excellence

    Our first step was the District 306 D8 orientation. This is only the beginning of Excellence.
