Our Club Advisor

Every Leo club has a Lion beside it. Ours is Lion Ravindu Runage of the Lions Club of Matara Excellence.

Lion Ravindu Runage MJF, PMAF

Club Advisor, Leo Club of Matara Excellence

Lion Ravindu belongs to the Lions Club of Matara Excellence, the club that sponsors us, and led it as President in 2025–2026. As our Club Advisor he is the link between the two clubs: he helps our officers plan, and makes sure a group of first-time leaders has someone experienced to turn to.

In Lions he also serves beyond his own club. For 2026/2027 he is a Zone Chairperson in Lions District 306 D8, for Region 04, Zone 10. A zone chairperson looks after the group of Lions clubs in a zone, visits them, and keeps them in touch with the district. He is a Melvin Jones Fellow (MJF), a recognition named after the founder of Lions.

He guides and encourages, and he leaves the deciding and the doing to the Leos.

What a Leo club advisor does

Lions Clubs International asks every sponsoring Lions club to appoint one of its members as the Leo club advisor. It is a mentor’s job, not a manager’s.

Guides the officers

Helps the President, Secretary and Treasurer learn their roles, run meetings and keep proper records.

Links the two clubs

Carries news, needs and ideas between the Leos and the Lions, so the two clubs can work side by side.

Stands with the club

Attends meetings and projects, looks out for the members, and takes part in installing each year’s officers.

The Lions Club of Matara Excellence

Our sponsoring club is one of the newer Lions clubs in Matara. Lions Clubs International records list it as chartered in September 2024, and it sponsored our Leo club soon after. Its members guide us, come to our events and cheer us on while we do the work ourselves.
