About the project

Leo Abhivandana (ලියෝ අභිවන්දනා) was an evening of reverence held at Sri Pushparama Pirivena at 6:30 PM on Vap Poya, 25 October. Its theme, “blessings in the shade of the Bo, for a new step”, said what the club wanted: to begin its journey with the blessings of the Sangha and the quiet of a temple evening.

Members gathered to pay respects, to reflect on why we serve, and to ask for strength for the work ahead. The project was successfully completed, and it set a calm, grateful tone for the club’s first season.

Why Vap Poya

Vap is the full-moon Poya of October. It marks the end of Vassa, the three-month rains retreat observed by the monks, who take part in the Pavarana ceremony, inviting one another to point out any fault. Vap Poya also opens the Kathina season, when lay devotees offer the Kathina robe to the Sangha, and Sri Lankans call the following month Cheewara Masaya, the month of the robe.

It is a day of ending and beginning, which is why we chose it for the club’s own new beginning.

Thank you

Thank you to the community of Sri Pushparama Pirivena for welcoming us, and to every member and friend who joined.
