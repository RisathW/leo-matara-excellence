Children and youth

Every child deserves a good start.

Helping children and the families who care for them, including those facing childhood cancer.

Lions global cause
02 / 21

Environment and sustainability

Look after the place we live in.

Beach clean-ups, tree planting, cutting down waste, and helping communities get clean water and power.

Lions global cause
03 / 21

Health and nutrition

Healthy people, stronger community.

Health screenings, school meals, food-safety talks and blood donation camps.

Lions global cause
04 / 21

Peace and culture

Many languages, one community.

Poster contests, cultural evenings and shared events that bring different communities together.

05 / 21

Sports and recreation

Play is part of growing up.

Tournaments, coaching, and repairing playgrounds and sports equipment.

06 / 21

Senior citizens

Time is the best gift.

Visiting elders, helping at elders’ homes and simply keeping them company.

07 / 21

Disability support

Make room for everyone.

Ramps, mobility and hearing aids, and sports days that include everyone.

08 / 21

Public relations

Good work should be seen.

Recording and sharing our projects so people know what we do and can join in.

09 / 21

Fundraising

Raise it, then spend it well.

Carnivals, raffles and sales that pay for our projects, with every rupee tracked back to its cause.

10 / 21

Education and literacy

Keep children learning.

Books, libraries and the small costs that keep a child in school.

11 / 21

Women empowerment

Skills and confidence for young women.

Vocational training, health and safety awareness, and leadership programmes.

12 / 21

Poverty and community development

Lift the whole neighbourhood.

Repairing homes and shared spaces, and giving rations and school supplies to families in need.

13 / 21

Safety and crime prevention

Safer streets, safer screens.

Road safety, first aid and online safety awareness.

14 / 21

Infrastructure

Build things that last.

Classrooms, water tanks, toilets and road lighting that serve a community for years.

15 / 21

Research and innovation

Find out what really works.

Surveys, pilot projects and competitions, and keeping records so we can measure our impact.

16 / 21

Drug prevention

Say yes to a clear future.

School programmes, and support for rehabilitation centres and the families affected.

17 / 21

Animals and wildlife

Kindness to every living thing.

Feeding, vaccination and sterilisation drives, plus reef and marine projects.

18 / 21

Fellowship and community engagement

Friends make the work last.

Joint projects, conventions and social events that keep members connected.

19 / 21

Hunger and food security

No one should go to bed hungry.

School meal programmes, food drives and flood relief.

Lions global cause
20 / 21

Eye screening camps, spectacles and eye-donation drives, building on the Lions’ long history in this field.

Lions global cause

Disaster management

Be ready, be first to help.

Quick response after floods and landslides, plus drills and readiness kits.
