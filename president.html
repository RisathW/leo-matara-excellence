From our President

President’s Message

“

As the President of the Leo Club of Matara Excellence, I proudly address our community at the very start of our journey in Leo District 306 D8. I am deeply grateful to our members, to the Lions Club of Matara Excellence who guide us, and to every Leo leader whose support has given this club its first strong steps.

Our legacy begins with Service & Success. Our first big day, the District 306 D8 orientation, showed what a group of young people can do when they decide to show up. My vision is to empower every member, to strive for excellence in all we do, and to build a lasting legacy of fellowship and service in Matara.

This is only the beginning of Excellence. Together, we will keep serving our town and growing as leaders, and I invite every young person who wants to make a difference to walk this road with us.

Leo Ramudi LiyanagePresident · Leo Club of Matara Excellence
